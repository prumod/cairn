Q1 · What is drawn under the markers? (throwaway prototype for «How is the places map drawn?»)

The same scene in all three: the company in Leiria (◆), a 50 km radius, and one marker per concelho in the place tree (29 concelhos). The number is the lotes count. The counts are fictional; 0 is dashed. The points and outlines are real CAOP2025 data, but they are a prototype approximation: an inner point per polygon (mapshaper), not the final #151 point. An amber ring marks a concelho whose own point is outside the circle while one of its freguesias is inside (Figueira da Foz at 51.7 km, Montemor-o-Velho, Óbidos, Constância, Abrantes at 69.4 km), as #210 requires. Hover a marker for its name and distance.

A · SVG + simplified CAOP outline

Concelho borders (thin), distrito borders (thicker), coast. No tiles, no map library.

  • Licence: CC BY 4.0, «© DGT, CAOP2025». We owe this attribution in every option anyway, because the points come from CAOP.
  • Cost 0, no key, no outside request. The company's area is never sent to anyone.
  • Whole Continente concelho outline at 2 % simplification: 59 KB gzip (TopoJSON), served as a static file.
  • No roads, towns or terrain.

B · MapLibre + OpenFreeMap tiles (OSM)

Real basemap: roads, towns, terrain labels. Pan/zoom built in.

The tiles did not load in this preview (no network or blocked). In a browser, B shows the OpenFreeMap «liberty» style.
  • Licence: ODbL data («© OpenStreetMap contributors») + OpenMapTiles + OpenFreeMap credit; the free host has no SLA.
  • Paid alternatives (MapTiler, Mapbox) need a key, quotas and terms review.
  • Adds MapLibre (~800 KB JS); every view requests tiles for the company's area from a third party.
  • Research: do not add a provider before its licence is proven.

C · Circle and markers only

No outline. The same points, with no geography under them.

  • Licence: the CAOP point attribution only.
  • Smallest and simplest.
  • The markers float in empty space: the coast, the borders and the shape of the region are gone.

The projection used in A and C is azimuthal equidistant, centred on the company. Every point is drawn at its true distance from the company, so the 50 km circle is exact and a concelho at 51.7 km sits visibly just outside it. Prototype only: prototype/201-places-map, never merged.
